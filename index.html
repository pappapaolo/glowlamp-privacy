Glow Lamp — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Glow Lamp does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

  • The app has no internet access, no accounts, no ads and no analytics or third-party SDKs.
  • It remembers only your chosen shape, colour palette, brightness and grain setting, saved on your device so the lamp looks the same next time. This never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.
  • To show true black around the light, Glow Lamp asks Android to hide other apps' screen overlays (such as colour filters) while the lamp is on screen. It does not read or change those apps; Android restores their overlays as soon as you leave Glow Lamp.
